Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026
This policy explains when a booking cancellation is eligible for a refund on QuickFit4u. It applies to all hourly gym session bookings made through our app.

Cancellation Window

✓
Cancelled 2 hours or more before your slot You'll receive a full refund of the amount paid, back to your original payment method.
✕
Cancelled less than 2 hours before your slot No refund is provided for late cancellations.
✕
No-shows If you don't check in for a confirmed booking and don't cancel it in advance, no refund is provided.

When You're Always Refunded, Regardless of Timing

If a confirmed booking cannot be honoured for reasons attributable to QuickFit4u or the partner gym — for example, the gym is closed, unavailable, or unable to accommodate your booking — you will receive a full refund or an alternative slot, at your choice, regardless of when this is identified.

How Refunds Are Processed

Eligible refunds are processed through Razorpay back to your original payment method (UPI, card, or netbanking). Refunds typically reflect within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

Rescheduling

Instead of cancelling, you can request to reschedule a confirmed booking to a different available time slot at the same gym, with a note explaining the reason. This request goes to the gym owner for approval and does not involve any refund or additional charge — your original booking remains valid and unaffected until the gym owner responds.
