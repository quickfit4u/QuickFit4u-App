Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026
Welcome to QuickFit4u ("QuickFit4u", "we", "us", or "our"). QuickFit4u is a platform that allows users to discover and book participating gyms and fitness centres for short-duration fitness sessions, including hourly bookings. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By using QuickFit4u, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

When you create an account or use our services, we may collect: full name, mobile number, email address, login information, profile information (including profile photo, gender, and address if provided), booking details, customer support communications, and any other information you voluntarily provide to us.

B. Booking Information

When you book a gym through QuickFit4u, we may collect and store the selected gym, booking date, booking time/session, booking ID, booking status, check-in information, reschedule requests, cancellation information, and refund information.

C. Payment Information

Payments are processed through our third-party payment service provider, Razorpay. QuickFit4u may receive transaction-related information such as transaction ID, payment status, payment amount, payment date, and payment method type. QuickFit4u does not store sensitive payment credentials such as your UPI PIN, card number, card PIN, CVV, or OTP — these are handled entirely by Razorpay.

D. Location Information

With your permission, we may collect location information to show nearby gyms and improve gym search results. You may disable location permissions through your device settings; however, some location-based features may not function properly without location access.

E. Gym Partner Information

If you register as a gym partner, we additionally collect gym details (name, address, facilities, photos, pricing), and bank account or UPI details for the purpose of processing your payouts.

F. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information, including device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and general usage/diagnostic information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to: create and manage your account; process and manage gym bookings; confirm payments and transactions; generate and verify booking details, QR codes, and check-ins; provide customer support; send booking confirmations and important service notifications by email; show gyms and services relevant to your location or search; process gym partner payouts; prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorised activity; improve our Platform and services; maintain business, transaction, and operational records; and comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Information Sharing

A. Partner Gyms

When you make a booking, relevant booking information (your name and booking details) is shared with the respective partner gym to enable booking verification, customer check-in, and support related to the booking. We do not share unnecessary personal information with partner gyms.

B. Service Providers

We use third-party service providers for payment processing (Razorpay), cloud hosting (Railway), email delivery (Resend), and technical infrastructure. These service providers process information on our behalf to provide their services and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

C. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, court order, or lawful government request.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction — including password hashing and encrypted connections. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are also responsible for keeping your account credentials and device secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for providing our services, maintaining booking and transaction records, customer support, security and fraud prevention, legal and regulatory requirements, and resolving disputes. When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to access certain personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion of your account or personal information where legally and operationally possible; withdraw certain permissions, such as location access; and contact us regarding privacy-related concerns.

7. Account Deletion

You may delete your QuickFit4u account directly from within the app (Settings → Delete Account). Upon deletion, we remove your profile, bookings, and reviews associated with your account. However, certain transaction records may need to be retained where required for legal compliance, financial records, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

8. Children's Privacy

QuickFit4u is not intended for use by individuals who are not legally permitted to use the Platform under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The Platform uses third-party services as described above. These third parties have their own privacy policies and terms, and QuickFit4u is not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services before using them.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Platform after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective will be subject to the updated policy.
